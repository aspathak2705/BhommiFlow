# 18. Data Provenance and Trust

## Hierarchy of Truth

```
1. Official Government Regulations (D4 Verified Manuals)
   ↓
2. Canonical Relational Database Records (PostgreSQL Cases & Parcels)
   ↓
3. Document Extractions & Evidence Provenance (D2 OCR Extractions)
   ↓
4. Calibrated ML Delay Probability (D5 Machine Learning Model)
   ↓
5. LLM Grounded Explanation & Synthesis (Zero Uncited Claims)
```

## Dataset Provenance Definitions
* **Dataset 1 (D1)**: Synthetic land acquisition case records derived from benchmark statutory fields.
* **Dataset 2 (D2)**: Synthetic OCR land document extractions (7/12 Extracts, Sale Deeds, Notices).
* **Dataset 3 (D3)**: Synthetic document pair comparison records and land record discrepancies.
* **Dataset 4 (D4)**: Validated RFCTLARR Act 2013 government procedure knowledge.
* **Dataset 5 (D5)**: Pre-trained machine learning model artifacts for statistical delay prediction.
