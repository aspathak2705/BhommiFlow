# 16. Pitch Master Script

## Timed Pitch Options

### 30-Second Pitch (Elevator)
"Land acquisition in India faces chronic overruns because evidence is fragmented across paper records, conflicting surveys, and departmental silos. BhoomiSakha unifies land evidence, uses a calibrated machine learning model to predict delay risks months in advance, grounds interventions in government procedure, and empowers officers with auditable decision workflows."

### 60-Second Pitch (Executive)
"Infrastructure projects lose thousands of crores to avoidable land acquisition delays. Current systems only digitize records — they don't predict risk. BhoomiSakha connects multi-source case data, extracted document OCR, and comparison mismatches into a unified evidence graph. Our Dataset 5 machine learning pipeline predicts delay probabilities without target leakage, while Procedure RAG retrieves exact legal rules under the RFCTLARR Act to recommend prioritized interventions. The nodal officer retains final decision authority, closing the loop with automated notification simulations and immutable audit trails."

### 3-Minute Pitch (Technical)
"BhoomiSakha is built on a 4-plane architecture separating Experience, Evidence, Intelligence, and Governance. 

In the Evidence Plane, Datasets 1, 2, and 3 structure case metadata, OCR extractions, and document mismatches into isolated vector chunks. In the Intelligence Plane, non-leaking project features pass through a Scikit-Learn pipeline to output calibrated delay probabilities and risk levels. 

In the Governance Plane, Procedure RAG retrieves verified Dataset 4 regulations to ground system recommendations. When an officer approves an intervention, our state machine advances the decision status, triggers a high-fidelity SMS/WhatsApp notification simulation, and creates an immutable audit record. BhoomiSakha delivers true decision intelligence."
